My story

I woke up after my burnout at 26. Before that I was living life to the max, a little too much to the max.

I was pleasing far too much and didn't know who I was at the core. I had to search for years for answers. Who am I? What am I doing here? Who are my people? What is my purpose?

I need freedom, my own direction, my own time. The moment I stop planning, I become most creative and everything literally comes toward me.

I feel others well, but now I let my own feeling come first. What I love most is helping people find who they are.
