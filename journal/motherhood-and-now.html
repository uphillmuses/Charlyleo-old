Motherhood, and now

By Charlotte

When my daughter was born, I knew I couldn't leave her at daycare. I became a full-time mother, staying home for the first four years of both children, while studying, designing our villa in the dunes of Zandvoort and raising two cats, two rabbits and an Australian Shepherd.

Those were intense years. I'm proud that I was with the children for so long, and they are amazing kids, but for me it had a price. I was still in the process of finding myself. Now that I know who I am, I would not do it the same way.

As a mom, your health and happiness should always come first.

When both kids went to school, calm finally returned, and then we moved to Bali. Now I give astrology readings, help people improve the energy in their homes and make all kinds of products. My life flows, I know what I want, I have amazing people around me, and life comes to me.
