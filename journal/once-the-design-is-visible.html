Once the design is visible, judgment gives way to clarity

By Charlotte

Understanding astrology clarified the patterns that shape my nature. I instinctively attune to others before myself. That is why I didn't know who I was: I was so in line with others that I could not feel me. Now that I know how to feel both, it makes me a good astrologer.

I am deeply content in solitude, yet love being in the right company. I am drawn to the spotlight, and that is fine, I belong there. Still, because of my rising sign, it can make me very uncomfortable.

Pleasing others has often been the path of least resistance. This life calls for something else: living according to my own direction. What may look self-centred to some is often simply a different design. That is the beauty of astrology: you understand all the little things that shape a person.
