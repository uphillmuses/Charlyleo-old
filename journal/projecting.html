Projecting

By Charlotte

When I was 26, I began the three-year Jungian therapy training with Karen Hamaker. I became fascinated by projection. I learned that every time an emotion or irritation arises because of what someone else is doing, the real work begins inside.

Everything that irritates us about others can lead us to an understanding of ourselves. Carl Jung

Over the past fifteen years I have worked with this so consistently that it has become one of the foundations of how I work as a coach, astrologer and medium. Because every chart is different, I rarely trust another coach or psychologist completely: most still project. I can usually see and feel when someone is doing it.

That is why feeling the other person clearly, and letting go of your own beliefs, matters so much in my work. As a Projector in human design, I can tap into someone else's energy quite easily, and I can steer it so my own beliefs move to the background. Still, I will ask you not to trust me completely either. Only you have your own answers.
